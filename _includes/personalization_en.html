1. Sizes & Scaling

Thanks to modern 3D printing, I am not bound to fixed dimensions. Almost every model can be scaled to your exact desired size – whether as a miniature for a display case or a masterpiece for your shelf.

Centimeter-Precise Flexibility

By default, my resizing is proportional to perfectly preserve the model's artistic design and aesthetics. Upon request, specific non-proportional adaptations are also possible (e.g., to make a model fit perfectly into a specific display cabinet or shelf niche).

  • 📐 Wide Range: From tiny collector's miniatures (e.g., 2 cm height) up to impressive 25 cm masterpieces for your shelf or sideboard.
  • 🔍 High Detail Fidelity: Even with highly downscaled decorative figurines, the fine structures and details are brilliantly preserved.
  • 🏠 Perfect Fit: Ideal to customize decorative objects for specific shelf compartments, shadow boxes, or display spaces.

3. Custom Engraving & Model Merging

I make your decorative prints unique. In addition to three-dimensional inscriptions, I offer precise digital merging of different display models, artistic pedestals, and decorative accessory parts.

3D Inscriptions & Nameplates

Unlike post-print engravings or stickers, your custom inscription is integrated directly in 3D during the printing process (embossed or recessed). This makes it highly wear-resistant, durable, and blends elegantly with the design.

  • ✍️ Individual Fonts: From cursive scripts to clean, modern block letters.
  • 🎁 Ideal for Occasions: Perfect for birthdays (e.g., with name & birth date), weddings, or anniversaries.
  • 💡 Endless Creativity: Integrate short quotes, dedications, or small symbols (like hearts or stars).

Model Merging & Custom Adaptations

Would you like to permanently place a figure on a specific base, merge two different 3D models into a new custom piece, or attach a pre-made accessory to your model? Instead of creating completely new objects from scratch, I focus on digitally combining and adapting existing decorative models and artistic shapes to perfectly match your vision.

  • 🧩 Model Merging: I digitally combine different 3D models (e.g., placing a figure permanently onto an elaborate scenic base, or merging two characters into a dramatic, custom display scene).
  • 🎩 Accessories & Attachments: Attaching pre-existing accessory parts like hats, glasses, or decorative elements directly to your favorite model before printing.
  • 🏛️ Thematic Display Pedestals: Combining organic figures with geometric pillars, custom nameplates, or decorative stands for a premium, museum-like presentation.
